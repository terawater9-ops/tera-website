About this tool

The ratings follow widely used irrigation water guidelines from the FAO (Ayers and Westcot) and US university extension services for drip clogging. They're general guidance: crops differ in how much salt and sodium they tolerate, and soil matters too. For a recommendation for your farm, send us your results. It's free.
