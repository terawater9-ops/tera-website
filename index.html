Problems we help growers solve

Most water problems on a farm show up in the field long before anyone tests for them.

Clogged drip lines and emitters

Uneven watering, dry spots, and hours spent flushing lines. Usually sand, iron, scale or algae.

Sand and silt from the well

Worn pumps, plugged filters and sprinklers. A sand separator or the right screen often solves it.

Iron and manganese

Orange or black stains and slime inside the lines. It needs treatment before filtration.

Salty or hard well water

Salt crust on the soil, leaf burn, lower yields. We check whether it can be managed or needs treatment.

Reusing tailwater

Capturing runoff for reuse, and cleaning it well enough to protect your irrigation system.

How it works

You deal with us for the diagnosis, and with an established supplier for the equipment. Everything is done by email, at your pace.

  1. Tell us what you're seeing

    Email us your location, water source, crop and irrigation type, with a few photos. Free.

  2. Test the water

    We tell you which irrigation test to order from an accredited lab. You mail the sample yourself. The lab charges you directly, usually under $100.

  3. Get a plain answer

    We read the report with you and explain what's causing the problem and what kind of fix makes sense. Free.

  4. Get a quote from a trusted supplier

    We introduce you to an established US manufacturer or dealer. They quote, sell, ship and stand behind the equipment.

The USDA may pay most of the cost

The USDA's Environmental Quality Incentives Program (EQIP) can cover a large share of irrigation improvements for eligible growers. Payment comes after the work is done, and you apply through your local NRCS office.

We explain how it works and put together a clear file for your application: the water report, the problem, the recommended fix and the supplier's quote.

Before you buy anything

If you plan to use EQIP, don't buy or install equipment before your contract with NRCS is signed. Approval is decided by NRCS, not by us or the supplier.

Larger projects

For larger farms that prefer to buy direct from a manufacturer overseas, we can introduce vetted factories and help coordinate third-party inspection. You remain the buyer and importer.

The right fix, not the biggest one

Many water problems are solved with the right filter or a treatment step, not a whole new system. We compare options and recommend the most cost-effective fix for your water, your crop and your budget.
